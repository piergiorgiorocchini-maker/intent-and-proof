Blaming every no-show on an unserious patient prevents the system from improving.

### Training and feedback

Marketing can use front-desk conversations as a research source, subject to privacy requirements. Repeated objections can become FAQs, landing-page sections and negative keywords. Questions the page fails to anticipate reveal information gaps.

The front desk should receive context about campaigns: which advertisements are active, which treatments are being promoted and what promise the patient has seen. Without that context, staff are responding to a conversation that began elsewhere without knowing its opening line.

Collaboration creates a loop:

**real questions → better content → better-informed enquiries → better conversations → new data**

That loop is one of the elements that separates a conventional agency from vertical acquisition infrastructure.

## CRM and dental practice-management software: connect marketing with operations without duplicating the clinical record

A dental practice already has a central system: its practice-management software. The diary, patient records, estimates, treatment plans, billing and clinical documentation normally live there. Building a parallel marketing CRM that copies everything increases duplication, error and risk.

The objective is not to replace the practice-management system. It is to create an integration layer that preserves the source of the enquiry and returns enough operational stages for measurement.

Products already on the market show that many of the required data points exist. Open Dental publishes a REST API with patient and appointment resources. OpenStudio describes patient, appointment, estimate and treatment-plan progress management. MedicHub presents scheduling, estimates, reports, KPIs and treatment profitability. FELG Dent includes calendars, treatment plans, estimates, reporting and a developer API proposition. CAES offers software for dental organisations and economic-management tools. [Sources: Open Dental API](https://www.opendental.com/site/apispecification.html), [OpenStudio](https://www.openstudio.cloud/gestionale-studio-dentistico), [MedicHub](https://www.medichub.it/gestionale-odontoiatrico), [FELG Dent](https://felgdent.com/it/), [CAES](https://www.caes.it/)

These pages describe features claimed by the vendors. They do not prove that every system provides a public, documented or suitable API for a specific implementation. Compatibility must be verified product by product and market by market.

### The Lead Gateway

Intent & Proof uses the term *Lead Gateway* for the component that receives the website enquiry, assigns an identifier, stores permitted marketing parameters and transfers the necessary data into the system selected by the practice.

The gateway should not become a clinical record. Its role is limited to:

- validating the form;
- recording consent and source;
- creating a `lead_id`;
- preserving UTM parameters and permitted technical identifiers;
- sending the contact to the CRM or practice-management system;
- recording delivery;
- receiving data-minimised operational stages.

This separation reduces coupling between the website and practice software. If the practice changes systems, the form and dashboard should not need to be rebuilt from zero. A new connector can be developed or configured instead.

### Minimum data model

The practice-management system may contain name, telephone number, email, appointments, documents and health information under the appropriate roles and legal bases. The attribution layer should contain far less.

A marketing record might contain:

```json
{
  "lead_id": "lead_7f31",
  "created_at": "2026-08-04T09:40:00+02:00",
  "source": "google_ads",
  "medium": "cpc",
  "campaign_id": "9876543210",
  "landing_page": "/dental-implants/",
  "location": "cagliari",
  "stage": "lead_created"
}
```

An event returned by the practice system might be:

```json
{
  "event_id": "evt_9021",
  "lead_id": "lead_7f31",
  "event_type": "first_visit_completed",
  "event_time": "2026-08-12T15:30:00+02:00"
}
```

A separate internal economic event may exist for the practice dashboard:

```json
{
  "event_id": "evt_9138",
  "lead_id": "lead_7f31",
  "event_type": "internal_plan_outcome",
  "status": "accepted",
  "value": 4500,
  "currency": "EUR"
}
```

That final event should not be uploaded automatically to Google or another advertising platform. It belongs to the practice's internal business intelligence and must comply with platform policy, privacy rules, access roles and defined purposes.
